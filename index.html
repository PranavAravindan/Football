🏈 QB Legend

Call the play, take the snap, read the field and beat the pass rush before you’re sacked.

Keyboard: Space = snap, then press the letter above a receiver to throw to him.
AR: allow the camera and stand back so both shoulders and your throwing arm are in view. After the snap, throw overhand like a real pass toward the screen – a swing tracker reads your arm’s speed and direction. Throw harder for a deeper pass. Run Train AR throws once so it learns your arm. You can over- or under-throw!
